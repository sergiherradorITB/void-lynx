# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Esports fans, team followers, and visitors looking for information on the Voidlynx roster, staff, and recent announcements.

## Product Purpose
An esports organization landing page that serves as a central hub. It provides quick shortcuts to social media (RRSS), introduces the team rosters and staff, and includes a blog for publishing announcements. Success means fans can easily follow the team and stay updated.

## Positioning
The official online presence and source of truth for the Voidlynx esports team.

## Operating Context
Accessed via web browsers on desktop and mobile devices, typically following links from social media platforms.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Built with TanStack Start, TypeScript, React, and Tailwind CSS.
- Must preserve the existing Voidlynx logo.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Voidlynx
- The existing logo is a strict brand commitment and must be preserved.
- The design must be extremely simple and minimalist, acting as a clean canvas for the logo rather than a complex visual world.

## Evidence on Hand
No existing real content, data, demonstrations, testimonials, or press provided yet.

## Product Principles
1. **Fan-Centric Hub**: Make social media shortcuts and team information the most accessible elements.
2. **Authoritative Source**: Serve as the official voice for Voidlynx announcements.
3. **Brand Continuity**: Ensure the visual identity revolves seamlessly around the existing logo.
